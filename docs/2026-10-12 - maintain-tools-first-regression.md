## The tool's finding

Every rep ran `--check` before writing (it exits saying no report exists yet), and three
shaped their report's wording around what it counts: a line reading "Held promotions: none"
would have counted as a held promotion and demanded a split-job line, "flag-code-bug: none"
likewise. The count was a word match. Fixed before the ship: a line that says none, no or
nothing beside the word does not count, and a third crafted fixture (`empty/`, a report with
nothing to persist) holds the expected output.
