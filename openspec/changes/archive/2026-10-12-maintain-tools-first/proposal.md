# Proposal: maintain-tools-first

## Why

The last of the three trims. MAINTAIN is 10.7 KB (2.7k tokens, 151 lines, 19 rules); its
weight is M17 restating `rollover.py` (1.7 KB), the sources and slices a worker is told to
assemble (M10, M11, M19's watermark arithmetic), and the report's required lines (M16) and
persistence lines (M13, M14, M15) stated as prose a rep historically omitted (PR3: "today's
reps' reports never carry the accounting"). Same direction as the SETUP and AUDIT changes:
detection and verification in a script, judgment in prose, a rule its action plus one
boundary.

## What Changes

- **`sweep.py` beside the skill, two modes.** Before the run: the live journals against
  their budget (M17), the rolled directories with the files newer than the last report's
  watermark (M19), the dated records, the archived designs (M11), the targets with their
  sizes and splits (M9, M14), a portfolio DOMAIN beside the ancestor router (M15), the last
  report, and the slices (M10). After the run, `--check`: the newest maintain report read
  back for the accounting slot's three lines (M16) and, when the report mentions them, the
  ROADMAP lines a code-bug flag (M13), a held promotion (M14) and a portfolio-level graduate
  (M15) require. Detection only.
- **SKILL.md rewritten to the actions:** about 8 KB from 10.7; M17, M19, M11, M10 and M16
  keyed to the script; the rationale parentheticals out.
- **A script-level check:** `sweep.py` byte-exact on the base TidePool and the `big-journal`
  and `fat-arch` variants (the sources mode) and on two crafted report fixtures (the check
  mode, one passing and one failing), under `harness/sweep-fixture/`.
- **A regression GREEN, not a RED:** five plan-only single-agent reps, one per family (the
  base TidePool's PR1 to PR3, CB1, CB2, CB4, O2 to O4; `fat-arch` FA1 and FA2; `big-journal`
  RO1 to RO5 as a plan; the reefstack PL1 from `ReefDash/` and PL2 with a portfolio DOMAIN
  present), Sonnet at high through a Workflow. A cell that fails names the clause restored.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `docs-architecture-maintain`: the sweep's sources and the report's check are scripted (a
  new requirement); the rules' wording, no requirement changed.

## Impact

The deployed footer and the spec's requirements stand; `rollover.py` and `journal_index.py`
are unchanged. With this change every skill of the family has its script and its text at its
actions.
