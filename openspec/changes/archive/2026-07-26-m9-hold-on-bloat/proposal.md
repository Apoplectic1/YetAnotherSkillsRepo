# m9-hold-on-bloat — proposal

## Why

Field RED (TSM maintain run, 2026-07-26, NOTEBOOK entry): the rep graduated into a
37.6→38.6 KB ARCHITECTURE.md **three times in one sweep, then asked** "split it?" — it knew
M9 (quoted "split before you stuff" in its own closing) and inverted the required order
under finish-the-graduate pressure. Stuff-then-ask compounds: every subsequent sweep fattens
the same target until someone splits it. M9 states the rule but gives no procedure for the
graduations themselves when the target is bloated, which leaves "apply now, flag after" as
an available rationalization.

## What Changes

- **MAINTAIN**: M9 hardened in place (R21-precedent: text sharpened, ID unchanged) with the
  explicit red-flag — *flagging the split after stuffing is the failure mode, not
  compliance* — plus a new **M14** hold procedure: a graduate whose charter-selected target
  is already bloated is **held**, not applied — recorded in the M13 report with its target +
  disposition, plus one ROADMAP open-line naming the pending split job; the split runs as
  its own adjudicated job (setup/audit territory per M9), and the held promotions land in
  the split homes afterward. No graduation content is lost (the report carries it) and no
  bloated doc gets fatter.
- **Fixture**: TidePool gains a fat-ARCHITECTURE variant (bloated reference doc + journal
  entries whose charter target is that doc) — new plant class; prompt-only probes per the
  t3/R21 precedent carry part of the RED (fat-router lesson: small synthetic may not
  reproduce; scale or scenario-framing must make the bloat unambiguous).
- **Gate (iron law)**: field RED is evidence but text ships only on synthetic RED→GREEN on
  non-derived fixtures (TSM is the deriving project — poisoned for this rule). No-failure →
  status-note.
